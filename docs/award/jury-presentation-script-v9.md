## Slide 7: Inside the Live CRM Machine & Behavioral Intent Escalation
*Presenter Actions: Transition to Slide 7. Point to the Heatmap and Duplicate Routing diagram.*

"Now, let's step on to the sales floor. When a real estate agent logs into a traditional CRM, their worst nightmare is duplicate leads—it causes commission disputes, client double-calling, and administrative chaos. 

But inside Entrestate, we have turned deduplication into a core competitive weapon. 

Our landing pages track client-side micro-behavioral telemetry: we calculate the precise coordinates of mouse movements, scroll-depth velocities, and active hover durations over pricing tables. Additionally, our lead forms utilize choice-engineered questions that classify a buyer's profile under the hood without asking a single direct question.

If a prospect registers for two different properties in the exact same community, our system computes a high Intent Convergence Index (ICI). The database instantly upgrades their status to a green Rate 8, places them at the very top of the broker's dashboard, and starts a 15-minute contact countdown. If the broker fails to respond in 15 minutes, the system redistributes the lead. 

But if they are just a scattered browser looking at a villa in the morning and a studio at night, their duplicate is merged silently without alerting the broker. We protect our sales floor's capacity by filtering out the noise while ensuring our hottest opportunities are called in minutes."  (Behavioral Intent Escalation)

## Slide 8: The Closed Retraining Loop (How Deals Teach Targeting)
* **Visual Setup on Screen**: Animated loop showing: 
  `Active Lead (Rate 8) -> CRM Won Status (Rate 9) -> Learning Engine (Weight Delta Calculation) -> Meta Ads Audience Refresh` [16, 17]
* **Timing**: `8:00 – 9:00` (60 seconds)
* **Speaker Script**:
  > "Let us look at how the loop formally closes when a transaction is completed.
  > 
  > When an agent successfully closes a contract, they mark the lead status as **Won** in the CRM [32]. The system automatically promotes that record's internal parameter from a Rate 8 to a gold **`Rate 9`**. 
  > 
  > This state transition is the programmatic trigger for our **Learning Engine (Engine 09)** [16, 17]. 
  > 
  > The engine evaluates the customer's profile, calculates a positive performance delta ($\\Delta_p$), and re-seeds your target lookalike audiences [16, 17]. 
  > 
  > By transforming a closed deal into training data, we ensure that next month's advertising budget does not chase generic demographics or ad-network guesses [31]. It programmatically pursues individuals who look exactly like your actual buyers [31]. This self-sharpening mechanism continuously lowers CPL and drives sales velocity over time."
