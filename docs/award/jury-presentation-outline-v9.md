### SLIDE 8: The Dynamic Retraining Loop: "Deals Teach Targeting"
*   **Visual Headline**: How Deals Teach Targeting
*   **Sub-Headline**: *Programmatically splitting active leads into Copy, Avoid, and Unrated Cohorts.*
*   **Key On-Screen Elements**:
    *   Diagram of the CRM-driven audience split:
        *   **`Rate >= 4`** ➔ **"Copy" Cohort** (114 Matched Lookalike Seeds programmatically pushed to Meta and Google marketing APIs).
        *   **`Rate 1–3`** ➔ **"Avoid" Cohort** (118 Junk Exclusions synced to suppress advertising waste).
        *   **`Rate null`** ➔ **"Unrated" Cohort** (1 Lead quarantined for manual agent review).
*   **Presenter Narrative**:
    "Here is the core intellectual property of Entrestate: **the rate is not a metric, it is a control signal**. 
    
    When an agent updates a lead's rating in the CRM, the system programmatically splits our active database. Our **`118 junk leads`** are compiled into an 'Avoid' Cohort and pushed directly to ad networks as custom exclusion lists—instantly shutting off advertising waste. 
    
    Meanwhile, our highly qualified prospects are compiled into a 'Copy' Cohort. Out of 122 qualified leads, **`114 high-integrity records`** were successfully resolved, deduplicated, and mapped straight to our Meta and Google custom audience APIs as seed files. When a deal is marked 'Won', it receives a **`Rate 9`**, triggering our Learning Engine to calculate performance deltas and retrain our lookalike definitions from scratch. Our deals literally teach our targeting."
